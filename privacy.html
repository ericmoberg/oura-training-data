Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Oura Training Data is a personal application used to retrieve and analyze data from the Oura API.

Data Access

The application may access Oura data authorized by the account owner, including health, sleep, recovery, activity, heart rate, workout, stress, and related Oura data.

How Data Is Used

Oura data is used solely for personal analysis, training tracking, recovery analysis, and visualization.

Data Storage

Data retrieved from Oura may be stored locally on devices controlled by the account owner. The application does not sell Oura data or use it for advertising.

Data Sharing

Oura data is not sold, rented, or intentionally shared with third parties.

Authorization

Access to Oura data is granted through Oura's OAuth authorization process. The account owner may revoke access through Oura's account settings.

Data Deletion

Locally stored data can be deleted by the account owner at any time. Revoking the application's access prevents future retrieval of Oura data.
